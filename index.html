About TR Signature Salon

Beauty, crafted with care and precision.

TR Signature Salon was created for the women of Vehari who want more than a routine appointment — a calm, welcoming space where hair, skin and makeup services are delivered with genuine attention to detail. Every visit is guided by clean tools, quality products and a team that listens first.

Whether you are here for a quick trim, a relaxing facial, or full bridal styling for your big day, our goal is the same: to help you look and feel like the best version of yourself.

  • Hygienic, comfortable salon environment
  • Experienced hair, skin & makeup specialists
  • Personalised bridal & occasion packages
  • Easy booking via call or WhatsApp
